# Surface Brief — Venue Big Screen (`/screen/[gameId]`) & Broadcast Leaderboard (`/screen/leaderboard`)

- **Visitor Mode:** Experience
- **Audience & Job:** Venue spectators and livestream audiences reading live match scores, dual-language segment titles, countdown timers, buzzer spotlights, and tournament standings from 10 meters away (`1920px–4K`, `16:9`/`16:10`/`21:9`), plus presenters operating the Big Screen alongside `/scorer/[gameId]` during the pitch to Marwan Serry and live tournament nights.
- **Confirmed Choices:**
  - Navigation chrome & Presenter Mode: Dedicated full-bleed broadcast canvas with no standard site navbar; a minimal auto-hiding floating control pill (Table Switcher, Leaderboard link, EN/AR toggle, realtime sync status, and Presenter Mode button) vanishes completely in Presenter Mode (Fullscreen API + cursor-none after inactivity, restored on mouse movement or `Esc`/`F`).
  - Leaderboard 10m strategy (`/screen/leaderboard`): Top-3 golden/crimson/blue Podium Spotlight paired with huge 10-meter legible rows with automatic/manual page cycling across all 27 players, round filter, and instant highlight/page-jump whenever a live `score.changed` event updates any contestant.
  - Winner celebration & Scheduled states: Baseline Framer Motion Victory Stage takeover when a game finishes (golden crown banner, elevated winner/champion card, animated final podium scores; Phase 10 confetti/sound/shake reserved behind `delightersEnabled`), plus a dramatic "Coming Up Next / VS Lineup" broadcast slate for scheduled tables like `game-14` Final that flips live automatically when the scorer starts the session.
- **Constraints:** EN (LTR) & AR (RTL) parity, `320px`–`2560px` & `4K` scaling without desktop TV scroll, `>= 44px` interactive control targets, no eyebrow kickers above headings, no blue directly on red without a crisp white buffer, `+`/`−` always paired with icons, no Phase 10 Delighters, Marwan Serry name only (zero photos), no copied show logo/text.
